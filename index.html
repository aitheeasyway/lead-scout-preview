FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need an X account?

No. It reads public posts. An X account only helps if you want to reply yourself.

Does it post or message people for me?

No. It only finds and suggests. It checks with you, and nothing posts or sends on its own.

Is this scraping a business directory?

No. It searches public X posts by topic or area, like you would, just faster and every day.

What does it cost?

The template and guide are free. It runs on Grok Bot, so you'll need your own Grok account.

I'm not techy. Can I do this?

Yes. That's the whole point of AI the Easy Way. Plain steps, no code.
